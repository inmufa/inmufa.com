A day at Unicorn Battle

On 29 January 2025 I went to Unicorn Battle at U-Enter, the business platform in Tashkent. It's a startup competition: teams get on stage and pitch, and the best ones win prize money. The teams were very competitive, and there were angel investors in the room.

Who I saw

I can't list every team, but a few stuck with me:

  • TurTopar, a travel-tech product for finding tours;
  • Tilmoch, an AI-powered translator built specially for Turkic and neighbouring languages;
  • Akam, a test-solving platform for universities that also helps applicants choose where to go.

The prizes

The teams in first, second and third place got prize money. Each of them had a few minutes to explain the product to people with real money to invest, and watching that taught me a lot about how to pitch: say the problem first, then the product.
