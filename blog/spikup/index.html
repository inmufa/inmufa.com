My first startup was about saying words out loud

Growing up in Central Asia, I saw how many people could read and understand English well and still struggled to speak it with confidence. I was one of them. I knew the feeling of mispronouncing a word, hesitating, and missing a chance to connect with someone.

The idea

There are few native speakers around here, so most of us never hear a new word said the right way. You can learn it and still not be sure how it sounds. SpikUp is my answer to that: an AI pronunciation coach on Telegram. You send a word, you hear it spoken by AI tutors, and you get the phonetic transcription to practice with. It takes a few taps.

Why it matters to me

SpikUp was my first step into the startup world. It taught me more than I expected about building something I believe in, and it set me on the path to everything I've started since. What I care about is simple: that more people can say what they mean clearly and take the opportunities that come with it.

If you'd like to try it, it's on Telegram at t.me/spikup_bot. I'd love to hear the first word you test.
